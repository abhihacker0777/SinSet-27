Call for Special Session Proposals is Open!

Special Sessions Call for Proposals

We invite academicians, scientists, researchers, industry professionals and subject experts to submit proposals for organizing Special Sessions at the 1st International Conference on Sustainable Innovations in Natural Sciences, Engineering and Technology (SINSET 2027).

Proposal Submission Deadline: 25 November 2026
Conference Dates: 27–28 February 2027
Venue: Poornima University, Jaipur (Hybrid Mode)

Overview & Purpose

The Organizing Committee of SINSET 2027 is pleased to invite proposals for Special Sessions to be included in the conference programme. The conference is organized by the Centre for Research, Instrumentation & Development (CRID) and the Faculty of Science and Humanities (FSH), Poornima University, Jaipur, in association with the Centre for Development of Physics Education (CDPE), University of Rajasthan, Jaipur.

SINSET 2027 aims to bring together leading academicians, scientists, researchers, industry professionals and practitioners from across the globe to share their latest research findings, innovations and technological developments in natural sciences, engineering, technology and interdisciplinary areas, with a particular emphasis on sustainable innovations.

The conference provides a multidisciplinary platform for discussing emerging developments in advanced materials, nanotechnology, life sciences, biotechnology, artificial intelligence, data science, smart manufacturing, renewable energy, sustainable engineering and other emerging technological domains.

Special Sessions are an integral part of the SINSET 2027 scientific programme. They provide opportunities to focus on emerging, specialized and interdisciplinary research themes that deserve dedicated academic attention. These sessions aim to facilitate focused technical discussions, encourage knowledge exchange, promote research collaborations and bring together experts working in specific areas of scientific and technological advancement.

Benefits of Organizing a Special Session

Recognition as a Special Session Chair

Opportunity to shape the technical content in your area of expertise

Networking opportunities with key researchers and industry professionals

Contribution to building a community around emerging research themes

One free registration per 8 accepted and registered papers, excluding the papers authored by the special session chairs (maximum 2 out of 8) in their respective special session.

Please Note: No TA/DA or accommodation is provided for special session organizers. They have to register and attend as the regular attendees only, but the registration fees can be waived off based on the criteria mentioned above.

Proposal Submission Guidelines

Special Session proposals must be prepared using the official proposal template and should include the following information:

  1. Title of the Special Session A clear, focused, and descriptive title reflecting the specialized theme.
  2. Organizers' Information Full names, institutional affiliations, designations, and contact details (email and phone) of all session organizers.
  3. Short Biography of Each Organizer A brief bio (100–150 words per organizer) highlighting academic credentials, research domain, and relevant experience.
  4. Session Abstract A detailed summary (300–500 words) explaining the significance of the topic, relevance to the scope of SINSET 2027, and its timeliness.
  5. Potential Contributors & Invited Speakers List of identified potential paper contributors, research groups, or invited keynote/session speakers.
  6. Expected Number of Papers & Speakers A minimum of 08 papers must be eligible for publication, and at least one keynote/invited speaker is expected per special session.

Review Process & Paper Format

All proposals will be evaluated by the conference's Special Sessions Committee based on their relevance, novelty, the profile of the organizers, and alignment with the overall goals of SINSET 2027.

Once accepted, special sessions will be listed on the official conference website, and a dedicated submission track will be created. Papers submitted to Special Sessions will undergo the same rigorous peer-review process as regular submissions and will be published in the conference proceedings, subject to the acceptance and registration of the authors.

Format for Paper Submission: 6 to 10 pages in MS Word with single line spacing, in Times New Roman font with font size 12 and page margin 1 inch for all sides.

The Microsoft CMT service was used for managing the peer-reviewing process for this conference. This service was provided for free by Microsoft and they bore all expenses, including costs for Azure cloud services as well as for software development and support.
